# Maple Espresso E-20

Counter espresso machine for a small café. Unplug before you open the tank.

## Daily clean
1. Purge the group head for 5 seconds.
2. Backflush with the blind basket and café cleaner, then rinse twice with water.
3. Wipe the steam wand and purge it until the steam is dry.

## Descale (every 30 days)
1. Empty the tank. Mix one packet of café descale powder with 1 litre of water.
2. Run half the tank through the group head, wait 15 minutes, then run the rest.
3. Rinse the tank and run two full tanks of fresh water through the group head.
4. Do not descale if the water filter was changed this week — rinse only.

## Water filter
Replace the in-tank filter every 60 days, or sooner if the flow slows.
