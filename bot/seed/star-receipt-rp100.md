# STAR RP-100 receipt printer

80 mm thermal printer under the counter. Use only 80 mm thermal paper.

## Paper jam
1. Open the clamshell lid. Do not pull paper out the front.
2. Lift the green release lever and ease the wad backward.
3. Tear a clean edge, drop the roll in so it feeds from the bottom, and close the lid until it clicks.
4. Press FEED once. If the light stays red, power-cycle the printer.

## Status lights
- Green: ready
- Amber: paper low
- Red: lid open, jam, or cutter error

## Cutter error
Open the lid, clear scraps from the cutter slot, close, then hold FEED for 3 seconds to reset.
