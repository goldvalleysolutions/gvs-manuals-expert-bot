# Counter tablet G3

Point-of-sale tablet on the stand. Keep it on the charger during service.

## Wi-Fi reset
1. Open Settings → Network.
2. Forget the shop network, wait 10 seconds, then join it again.
3. If it still fails, reboot the tablet from the power menu (not a hard shutdown).

## Frozen register
Hold the power button for 12 seconds until the screen goes black, then tap power once.
Do not pull the USB cable while a ticket is open — that drops the last order.

## Firmware
Shop builds ship on firmware 3.2. Only apply an update the owner downloaded; this
box does not fetch updates from the web.
