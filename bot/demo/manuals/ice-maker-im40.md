# Ice maker IM-40

Under-counter ice maker. Unplug before you reach inside the bin.

## Weekly sanitize
1. Discard remaining ice.
2. Mix 1 tablespoon of unscented bleach in 4 litres of water.
3. Wipe the bin, the scoop, and the door gasket. Rinse with clean water.
4. Run one harvest cycle and discard that first batch.

## Slow ice
Check the door seal and the intake filter. A dirty filter is the usual cause.
Do not chip ice with a knife — use the plastic scoop only.

## Scale on the evaporator
If cubes look cloudy and stick, run the descale cycle in the service menu
(hold CLEAN for 5 seconds) with the café descale packet, then two rinse cycles.
